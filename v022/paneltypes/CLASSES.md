# Panel type classes (same definitions as the v0.1/v0.2 analysis)

Classify each panel image into exactly one class:
- **micrograph**: a real image of the sample: SEM, TEM, HRTEM, STEM/HAADF, SAED or other diffraction patterns shown as images, EBSD/orientation maps, EDS elemental maps, optical/light micrographs, photographs of specimens.
- **spectrum**: a measured spectrum or diffraction profile plotted as intensity vs energy/angle/wavenumber/q: XRD, XPS, FTIR, Raman, EDS spectrum, XANES/EXAFS, SAXS/SANS profiles, NMR, UV-vis, also 2D maps of such data vs time.
- **trace**: a raw, non-spectral instrument curve: TGA/DSC, sorption isotherms, CV, charge-discharge curves, stress-strain or load-displacement curves, creep curves, impedance (Nyquist) plots, a direct time series from one measurement.
- **generated**: a derived or computed figure: a property plotted against a parameter across samples or conditions (e.g. hardness vs composition, conductivity vs temperature from many measurements), bar charts, histograms/distributions, fitted or simulated curves, calculated band structures, phase diagrams, schematics and cartoons.
If a panel combines types, choose the one that carries most of the panel.
